# AtlasOS project license scope

**Project license:** MIT, selected by the project owner for AtlasOS-owned source code.

The repository-root `LICENSE` applies to AtlasOS-owned source unless a file, component, asset, or distribution unit carries a separate notice or is excluded from publication. It does not transfer ownership of third-party material, grant trademark rights, or override upstream terms.

- The `atlas-boot` Rust crate retains its declared `MIT OR Apache-2.0` dual license. That declaration remains scoped to the crate and its eligible contributions.
- Ubuntu and Debian packages remain under their individual upstream licenses. Their package copyright records are the source of package-specific license information.
- Ubuntu Sans retains the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 notice in `atlas-boot/fonts/source/LICENSE-Ubuntu.txt`; it is not relicensed under MIT.
- Icons, logos, wallpapers, generated art, audio, and screenshots have independent provenance and rights statuses in [the asset inventory](asset-inventory.json). Unknown assets are excluded from the first-publication manifest until cleared or replaced.
- Product, service, and vendor names/logos remain subject to their owners' trademark policies. MIT grants no trademark license.

The root license is a project-owner decision, not a legal determination for third-party or generated material. See the [package distribution policy](package-distribution-policy.md) and [third-party notices](../release/third-party-notices.md).
